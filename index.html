Stick-figure answers to the questions you never thought to ask

Curiosity Drawn is a small educational animation channel on YouTube. Each video takes one everyday question, such as how people found their way before maps or what they did before mirrors, and answers it with researched facts and simple stick-figure animation. The sources are listed in every video's description.

Watch Curiosity Drawn on YouTube

Curiosity Drawn Publisher

The channel owner publishes the channel's own videos with a small private tool, Curiosity Drawn Publisher. It uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, with the title, description and visibility the owner chooses, and to show the owner whether an upload arrived. It is not a public service: only the channel owner can use it.
